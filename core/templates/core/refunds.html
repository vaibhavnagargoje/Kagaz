Refund & Cancellation policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Kagaz Plus (monthly or yearly) and conversion packs are one-time payments. Nothing renews automatically, so there is no subscription to cancel.

Refunds within 7 days

If you're not happy, ask within 7 days of paying and we'll refund the full amount. Write to us{% if business.email %} at {{ business.email }}{% else %} through our contact page{% endif %} with the WhatsApp number you used and the date of payment. Your Plus or pack stops when the refund is made.

Charged twice, or paid but nothing happened

If you were charged more than once, or paid and your plan didn't start, contact us. We'll start your plan or refund the extra payment in full, whenever you tell us.

After 7 days

Payments can't be refunded after 7 days. Your Plus keeps working until its end date, and pack conversions stay available.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days after we approve them, depending on your bank.

Stopping Kagaz

To stop using Kagaz, just stop sending messages, or type DELETE MY DATA to erase everything. Deleting your data doesn't refund a payment by itself: ask within 7 days if you want one.
